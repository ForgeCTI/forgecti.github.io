ForgeCTI

A Cyber Threat Intelligence framework bridging the gap between analysis and remediation, empowering organizations to forge a proactive defense against evolving cyber threats.

Thesis Proposals

Different Bachelor's and Master's thesis are available within the project, covering a wide range of topics in the field of Cyber Threat Intelligence, from data collection and analysis to visualization and reporting.

A list of publications related to the ForgeCTI project, including research papers, articles, and conference presentations, showcasing the latest advancements and findings.
